How it works

📷

1. Capture live

Take a photo with your camera. The GPS location is added automatically. No gallery uploads, so no fake reports.

🌐

2. Public and anonymous

Your name is never shown. Every issue is visible to everyone, so it cannot be ignored. Repeat reports add weight.

🛠️

3. Fixed and proved

The responsible official fixes it and uploads a verified "after" photo from the same spot. Only then is it marked Solved.
